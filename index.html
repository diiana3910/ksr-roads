About

Knowledge Source on Roads is a digital reference library maintained by Border Roads Task Force 763, Border Roads Organisation, Ministry of Defence, Government of India.

Documents are provided for reference. Copyright of each code or manual remains with its publisher (IRC, BIS, MoRTH, AASHTO, FHWA and others).
